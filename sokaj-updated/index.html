⚡01 — عن الفرقة

RAW.
LOUD.
STREET.

Sokaj started the way most things that matter start in Amman — loud, late, and out of a bedroom, not a studio. What came out was rough on purpose: distorted guitars, street Arabic, and lyrics that sound like they were overheard on a service taxi at 2am.

Four tracks in, the sound hasn't been sanded down. 6L3 Brasi runs on gasoline and bad decisions. 7asis Eni is a gas canister about to go off. Meen Had closes its jaws slow, like the flytrap on the cover. El 3alam El A7mar burns the whole thing down.
